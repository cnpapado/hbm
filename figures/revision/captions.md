**design_space.pdf.** The four evaluated designs for N = 16 logical data qubits, drawn from the simulator's layouts. Rows: T-state source (15-to-1 distillation factories or magic-state cultivation). Columns: placement of the sources, in the data plane or on an upper layer connected by vertical couplings. Planar-2D places a factory port on every other tile of a ring around the data; 2D-Cult places a cultivation patch beside each data qubit; HBM and HBM-Cult place one source above each data qubit (Dedicated) on an upper layer whose other tiles route T states (Upper-First). Factory bodies (11 tiles each) are not drawn; a cultivation patch is drawn as one tile. Shared-S layouts keep one upper-layer source per S data qubits.

**cross_layer_merge.pdf.** Lattice-surgery merge that injects a T state from the upper layer (d = 3). (a) The merged patch: data patch D, gap column G prepared in |+>, and T-state patch M; the Z checks that touch G are new during the merge and their product is Z_D Z_M. (b) The same checks folded at the seam: D on the lower layer, G and M on the upper layer with M directly above D. Only the checks outlined in magenta involve both layers. (c) The CNOT order of a cross-layer Z check and X check, measured by an ancilla on the lower layer; magenta CNOTs use a vertical coupling.
