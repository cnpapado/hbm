**design_space.pdf.** The four evaluated designs for N = 16 logical data qubits, drawn from the simulator's layouts. Rows: T-state source (15-to-1 distillation factories or magic-state cultivation). Columns: placement of the sources, in the data plane or on an upper layer connected by vertical couplings. Planar-2D places a factory port on every other tile of a ring around the data; 2D-Cult places a cultivation patch beside each data qubit; HBM and HBM-Cult place one source above each data qubit (Dedicated) on an upper layer that holds only sources. Factory bodies (11 tiles each) are not drawn; a cultivation patch is drawn as one tile. Shared-S layouts keep one upper-layer source per S data qubits, and T gates reach them by routing on the data layer to the tile below a source (Lower-First).

**lower_first.pdf.** Lower-First routing in a Shared-2 configuration of a Square Sparse layout with four logical data qubits (the simulator's layout): (a) both layers, (b) the data layer from above. The upper layer holds only the two magic-state resources: M1 above the ancilla tile between q1 and q2, and M2 between q3 and q4, each with one vertical coupling to the tile below it. A T gate on q2 routes over free ancilla tiles of the data layer (the T path), starting next to q2's Z boundary, to the tile below M1, and merges up through that tile's vertical coupling.

**cross_layer_merge.pdf.** Lattice-surgery merge that injects a T state from the upper layer (d = 3). (a) The merged patch: data patch D, gap column G prepared in |+>, and T-state patch M; the Z checks that touch G are new during the merge and their product is Z_D Z_M. (b) The same checks folded at the seam: D on the lower layer, G and M on the upper layer with M directly above D. Only the checks outlined in magenta involve both layers. (c) The CNOT order of a cross-layer Z check and X check, measured by an ancilla on the lower layer; magenta CNOTs use a vertical coupling.
